## What is SignalPGx?

**SignalPGx is white-label pharmacogenomics (PGx) reporting software for CLIA clinical laboratories.** It turns genotype data, medication history, and clinical evidence into physician-reviewed, EHR-integrated PGx reports delivered under a lab's own brand — so a laboratory can offer PGx reporting without building software or hiring bioinformaticians. A CLIA lab can launch branded PGx reporting in days.

Coverage spans **50+ pharmacogenes** and **~950 medications** with clinical PGx guidance, reconciled with source and version provenance on every gene–drug recommendation.

---

## 🧬 Why laboratories choose SignalPGx

- **Launch in days, not quarters** — no software to build, no bioinformatics team to hire.
- **Your brand, end to end** — reports go out under your laboratory's name, not ours.
- **Physician-reviewed by design** — every recommendation is drafted with citations, then reviewed and owned by a licensed clinician before release, with an optional clinical pharmacist (PharmD) or Medical Director overread step.
- **Reimbursement built in** — CPT + ICD-10 coding, MolDX Z-code context, and audit-ready medical-necessity documentation on every report.
- **EHR-native delivery** — results reach clinicians through HL7 FHIR R4, SMART on FHIR, and CDS Hooks.
- **Current by design** — coverage is continuously re-evaluated as guidelines and allele definitions change.

> **The flow, end to end:** genotype in → evidence reconciled with source + version → SignalAI drafts a source-cited interpretation → a licensed clinician reviews and signs → your branded report → delivered to the EHR.

## 📚 Evidence & clinical governance

Every gene–drug recommendation is reconciled across established public sources, with the **source and version recorded as provenance** on each recommendation — so a reviewer can trace exactly which guideline and allele definitions a result came from.

| Source | Role in the report |
| --- | --- |
| **CPIC** | Pharmacogenomic prescribing guidance |
| **DPWG** | Pharmacogenomic prescribing guidance |
| **FDA drug labels** | Regulatory pharmacogenomic labeling |
| **PharmGKB** | Curated gene–drug knowledge |
| **PharmVar** | Star-allele / haplotype definitions |
| **ClinVar** | Variant clinical significance |
| **ClinGen** | Gene and variant curation |
| **RxNorm** | Normalized drug nomenclature |
| **DailyMed** | Structured drug product labeling |

*Billing and reimbursement coding: CPT · ICD-10 · MolDX Z-codes.*

### Human-in-the-loop, clinician-owned

Every recommendation is **drafted with source citations, then reviewed and owned by a licensed clinician before release.** SignalPGx supports clinical decisions; it does not make them. **Prescribing decisions stay with the treating physician.**
